08 · TRACEABLE Every number the app asserts is shown being derived, including the rounding it does elsewhere. Charge history is bars, not a chart — orange marks only the charges that changed.
Decisions this flow takes
Privacy is loud, then quiet Front and centre once, before the first file, then it recedes to a line on the result screen and a settings entry. Repeating it every screen would read as protesting too much.
Savings framed as current overlap "$318/yr visible in what you've imported", not "found so far". Cumulative is more motivating and easier to overstate; this app can't afford a number it can't defend.
Low confidence still shows Below ~70% a finding loses its fill, its accent and its buttons — it becomes an observation with a percentage, ranked last. It never gets a notification.
Dismissal is a preference "I want both" reads as a choice, not a delete. It hides the pairing permanently but keeps both subscriptions in the list and their money in the totals.
