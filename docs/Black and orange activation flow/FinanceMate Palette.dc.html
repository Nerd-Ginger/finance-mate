FinanceMate · Colour

Black & orange

A dark-first, warm-neutral system. Black carries the interface; orange is spent only on money the app has found for you, and on the one action per screen. Everything else is neutral.

On-orange Text and icons on Primary are #0A0908, never white. Orange is a light colour; white on it fails contrast at body sizes.
03 — Meaning What a colour is allowed to say
Found money #FF7A18
Duplicates, price rises, avoidable fees, the what-if total. Orange means the app noticed something.
Money out #F2EDE8
Ordinary spending is neutral. Negative sign carries the meaning; colour would make every purchase feel like a warning.
Money in #5FB980
Positive amounts only. The single non-orange hue in the system; muted so it never competes.
Destructive #E2543F
Delete everything, undo an import, remove an account. Reserved for actions that lose data — never for spending.
Uncertain #332F2C
Low-confidence findings drop out of orange into outline-only neutral. Confidence is encoded as saturation, not as a badge.
Leaves the device outline
Egress log, payload preview, AI toggles. A hairline orange rule marks anything network-touching, on black, in mono.

06 — Rules
Do
Don't
One orange element per screen region — the finding, or the action, not both
Orange headers, orange dividers, orange icons everywhere
Fade confidence by removing colour: solid fill → outline → dashed outline
A separate warning hue for maybes — it reads as an error, not a guess
Flat black surfaces; separate by hairline or one step of lightness
Gradients, glows, or orange drop shadows on cards
Treat this as the default palette; let dynamic colour replace it wholesale if the user's wallpaper drives M3
Hard-code orange into logic that also has to work under a generated palette
